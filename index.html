About Me

My name is Alyssa Noelle, but I simply go by Aly. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies (BAMS) at the University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU).

While I have explored various creative fields, music has become my primary creative focus. I write, produce, record, and release my own music under the artist name ALY OCAMPO. I also work as a songwriter, producer, and artist for the indie P-pop group FRUITEEZ.

Beyond my independent music endeavors, I am an active member of UP MUSIKATS and The Feast PICC Youth Worship and Production Teams. I also work as a music mentor, coaching aspiring young artists in singing and music.

Passion Projects

Original Music and Songwriting

One of my main passion projects is creating original music. I write, produce, record, and release my own songs as ALY OCAMPO. Through songwriting, I get to turn my experiences and emotions into music and share my stories with other people.

FRUITEEZ

I am a member of FRUITEEZ, an indie P-pop group where I contribute as a songwriter, producer, and artist. This project allows me to collaborate with other artists and explore music as part of a group.

Music Mentoring

Through music mentoring, I get to share what I have learned with aspiring young artists. I help them explore singing, music interpretation, and songwriting while encouraging them to develop their own creativity.
